Continuous Dynamical Systems with Several Variables

Systems of differential equations, phase plane, linear systems, Michaelis–Menten kinetics, stability, and limit cycles. For each section there are the programs for all computed figures, apps to try in the browser, and the MATLAB apps of the course.

  • 4.1Systems of Differential EquationsSeveral quantities that influence each other lead to a system of differential equations. Its solution is viewed as time courses or as an orbit in the phase plane, where the direction field determines the motion. The programs solve the systems with solve_ivp (Python) or ode45 (MATLAB), or with Euler's method.6 figs. · Apps
  • 4.2Linear SystemsFor linear systems dx/dt = A x, the eigenvalues of A yield the solutions. With two variables, trace and determinant decide whether the origin is a node, a spiral, a center, or a saddle and whether it is stable. An example from pharmacology is the uptake of a drug from the stomach into the blood.4 figs. · Apps
  • 4.3Michaelis–Menten KineticsAn enzyme E binds a substrate S to form a complex SE, which decays into product P and free enzyme. The law of mass action gives a system for four concentrations. If little enzyme is present, the complex quickly settles at a quasi-steady value, and one obtains the Michaelis–Menten rate.4 figs. · Apps
  • 4.4Steady States and StabilitySteady states lie at the intersections of the nullclines. Their stability follows from the eigenvalues of the Jacobian matrix, that is, from the linearization. Examples are predator and prey, competing yeasts, the bioreactor with the washout of the biomass, and limit cycles in gene regulation and in the pacemaker tissue of the heart.6 figs. · Apps
  • 4.5In Depth: Excitability in the Phase PlaneWith h as the second variable, the Mitchell–Schaeffer model from Section 3.5 becomes a system in the phase plane. Nullclines and direction field explain threshold, action potential, and recovery. A constant inward current J0 makes the resting state disappear: a limit cycle arises, the model of a pacemaker cell.2 figs. · Apps
