Chapter 5 · Outlook: Propagation of Excitation in Cardiac Tissue

5.5From Tissue to the Whole Heart

In the heart, excitation spreads in three dimensions, faster along the fibers, whose direction rotates through the heart wall. Current heart models combine detailed cell models, the bidomain model, and patient-specific geometries; the simulation in Figure 5.7 of the book was computed with the freely available software openCARP on a laptop.

For each figure of the section you will find the program that produces it. Change parameters and initial values and observe what happens. Figure and equation numbers refer to the book.
